Support

For a gameplay bug or accessibility issue, open a public GitHub issue. Include your browser, device type, yard number, what you expected, and what happened. Do not include private information.

Private security reports

Report a suspected vulnerability through a private GitHub security advisory. Please allow reasonable time for investigation before public disclosure.

Quick recovery

  • Reload once if the physics engine does not start.
  • Open Sound to independently disable music, effects, or rider reactions.
  • Use Reset Progress at the bottom of the yard chooser if saved campaign data becomes confusing.
  • If the installed app seems stale, close it completely and reopen it online once.

Studio information is available at knockonwood.studio.
