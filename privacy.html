Privacy

Last updated August 25, 2026

Ride My Line is designed to work without an account. We do not use advertising trackers, cookies, or a persistent player identifier.

What stays on your device

Your unlocked yards, medal results, last-played yard, and sound preferences are stored in your browser. You can erase campaign progress from the yard chooser, or clear all game data through your browser settings.

Anonymous game events

We collect a small set of first-party events to find crashes and tune difficulty: game and yard starts, attempts, outcomes, failure categories, run duration, ink percentage, stars, checkpoint count, broad input method, and build version.

These events do not include your name, email, account, exact location, IP address, referrer, browsing history, free-form text, or a session or device identifier. The game does not send them when your browser enables Global Privacy Control or Do Not Track.

Infrastructure and retention

Cloudflare hosts the game and stores the aggregate game-event dataset for three months. Cloudflare may process ordinary request information to deliver and protect the service; operational Worker logs follow Cloudflare’s shorter plan retention limits.

Questions

Use the private security-reporting route linked from our support page for a privacy or security concern. Do not post private information in a public issue.
