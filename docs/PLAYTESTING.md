# Playtest protocol

Run this protocol before adding a yard to the release campaign and after any physics, input, camera, or scoring change.

## Cohort

Recruit at least 12 people who have not watched the yard being solved. Include touch-first and mouse-first players, at least two keyboard-only runs, and a mix of players familiar and unfamiliar with physics games. Do not coach after the opening prompt.

## Session

1. Ask the player what they think the green dashed box, red crossed box, pins, rings, star, ink bar, and flag mean before they act.
2. Have them play Yards 1–3 without intervention, then the current advanced slice.
3. Record yard, attempts to first clear, elapsed time, failure category, whether the same line shape was reused, star choice, and any moment of hesitation or surprise.
4. Ask what felt fair, what felt arbitrary, which failure made them want one more try, and whether sound helped them understand motion.
5. Test once with sound off and once with reduced motion enabled.

## Acceptance bands

- At least 80% correctly explain the green draw zone after Yard 2 and the red no-ink zone after Yard 3.
- At least 70% clear each of Yards 1–3 within five attempts without coaching.
- Advanced yards must not have a zero-clear cohort and should generally land between 15% and 60% first-session clear rate.
- No single generic stroke may clear more than half of Yards 4–25; the automated repeated-swoop sample must remain at zero clears.
- At least 80% can retry or reset without asking.
- Every failure must be explainable from the visible rule and result copy.
- No repeated report of nausea, unreadable focus, blocked zoom, inaudible critical feedback, or an unreachable 44px target ships unresolved.

## Decision log

Add dated results to the release issue, including cohort size, device mix, yard-level conversion, top three failure reasons, observed confusion, changes made, and the validation build. Never record player names in game telemetry.
